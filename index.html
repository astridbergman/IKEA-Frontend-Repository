FairSource A healthy supply chain across the world

Are you our audience?

Our product is targeted towards employees at IKEA who seek to keep track of working conditions in China when planning production in the country, so that they can minimize harm done to workers.

The product allows employees to easily visualize the working conditions in each region in the country, so that they can quickly and effectively gauge whether to accept business in a region.

This product aligns with IKEA:s goal to reduce inequality in its chain of production. It helps IKEA become more aware of how labour rights look in the regions it seeks to do business in, and thus allows them to make informed decisions that align with their values.

Five Dimensions

Individual Dimension

FairSource positively affects individual workers. By allowing IKEA to make informed decisions regarding which regions to purchase from, it incentivizes production facilities across the country to improve their standards.

This naturally benefits the workers at those factories. Workers live healthier lives, follow stricter safety standards and have more spare time to pursue other goals.

Social

FairSource gives workers more free time due to an eased workflow and a close eye on their good treatment, ensuring no region falls behind in wellness.

The result is happier workers that lead to a happier society, with an effectivized and healthy worklife.

Environmental

FairSource helps ensure that any improper treatment of environmental factors are dealt with, minimizing pollution by making the subject of pollution more serious.

Economic

FairSource lead to happier workers, which leads to a more motivated and healthy workplace. Production and standards increase the more wellness increases.

By ensuring that a nation has humane treatments in every region, its local economies are enforced, leading to more sales and prosperity.

Technical

FairSource's implementation is easy and quick to understand, making it very easy to add more sources than just a singular nation. Its use is relevant anywhere across the world with a workplace.

To use FairSource for more than just a single country is the ultimate end goal, and one that is not far from our grasp.

Five Dimensions of Sustainable Development

FairSource being fully sustainable in every aspect is one of our key agendas. As such, we have mapped the five dimensions in which FairSource is beneficial and sustainable on an individual, social, environmental, econonmic, technical and individual level.
